## Component and content specification

- `InsightArticle` now includes long-form sections, board questions, related-tool routing, source records, methodology, data period, and confidence.
- Article cards remain data-driven; the home page deliberately shows only three featured items.
- Full Analysis and Evidence View expose the long-form sections and framework.
- The provenance drawer renders dated source notes and external links separately from Ganrich interpretation.
- Every article closes with board questions and a contextual Decision Lab link.
- Article metadata, Article schema, canonicals, and EN/TR hreflang are generated from the selected record.
- The About page emits a sourced `Person` record for Ahmed Furkan Gul.
- No model-authored framework SVG was added; the existing restrained CSS framework language is retained.

## Owner decisions and missing evidence

1. **Engagement vignettes — blocking for client proof.** Provide four to six real engagements using: sector, region, situation, decision at stake, work performed, and what became measurable. Client names can remain confidential. Nothing will be inferred from general experience.
2. **Legal identity — blocking for contact and legal completion.** Confirm the registered entity name, registered address, registration identifiers, telephone number, and formal KVKK/GDPR rights channel.
3. **Response-time commitment.** Approve an operating promise only if inquiries can be monitored consistently.
4. **Editorial approval.** Confirm that the six founder-authored perspectives and their 24 July 2026 publication date may be made public.
5. **Founder image.** Supply an approved professional portrait if the monogram treatment should be replaced. No image was taken from LinkedIn.
6. **Newsletter provider.** Phase 1’s ESP, sender-domain, DPA, and double-opt-in decision remains open.
