# Ganrich Strategic Intelligence Environment

## 1. Expanded strategic concept

Ganrich now operates as a **digital strategic intelligence environment** organized around a four-part executive sequence:

**Signal → Interpretation → Choice → Action**  
**Sinyal → Yorum → Tercih → Uygulama**

The public experience creates useful value before a consulting engagement: it helps a visitor recognize an issue, frame the underlying decision, separate evidence from interpretation, compare alternatives, preserve material in a private local briefing, and decide whether a senior discussion is warranted.

The visual language remains quiet and editorial. Tools behave like executive working documents, not calculators or consumer quizzes. AI is used only where synthesis, retrieval, or critical review adds value; deterministic scoring remains visible and separate.

## 5. Public page specifications

Each row uses the sequence: **Need / Strategic purpose / Business value / Input / Output / Interaction / Lead role / Content / Data / AI / Privacy / Accessibility / Mobile / Phase**.

### Decision Atlas / Karar Atlası

- Need: find relevant Ganrich material through the decision, not a service taxonomy.
- Purpose: turn an executive question into a shareable decision pathway.
- Business value: high-intent discovery and tool/insight routing.
- Input: decision theme, region, urgency, maturity; later role, industry, constraint, horizon.
- Output: relevant expertise, region, tool, next question, and shareable URL state.
- Interaction: accessible radio list plus a visual decision network.
- Lead role: continues into a tool or private briefing after value is shown.
- Content: approved expertise, regional summaries, tool metadata, workshop metadata.
- Data: no external data required for routing.
- AI: none required; future natural-language classification may be AI-assisted.
- Privacy: URL contains non-confidential selections only.
- Accessibility: visualization has an equivalent structured list.
- Mobile: controls stack above the result; no hover-only nodes.
- Phase: 1.

### Signal Room / Sinyal Odası

- Need: understand a potentially important development without consuming a generic news feed.
- Purpose: connect evidence maturity and uncertainty to a leadership question.
- Business value: repeat readership and insight-to-conversation conversion.
- Input: region/theme/time-horizon/evidence-maturity filters.
- Output: signal, implication, uncertainty, decision to review, sources.
- Interaction: filterable editorial records, provenance control, save action.
- Lead role: source view → related insight/tool → private briefing.
- Content: signal owner, author, reviewer, interpretation, open question, translation.
- Data: dated approved sources and retrieval dates.
- AI: optional source clustering; never autonomous publication.
- Privacy: public content only.
- Accessibility: text confidence label, no color-only meaning.
- Mobile: single-column signal structure.
- Phase: 1.

- Interaction: tabs and structured timelines; no science-fiction imagery.

- Privacy: public input should remain non-confidential.

## 6. Interactive tool specifications

### Decision Framer / Karar Çerçeveleyici — implemented

- Need: turn a broad priority into a precise decision.
- Input: ten questions covering decision, urgency, outcome, alternatives, constraints, assumptions, uncertainty, stakeholders, delay, and evidence.
- Output: editable decision statement, desired outcome, alternatives, assumptions, evidence, stakeholders, delay risk, preliminary classification.
- Scoring: none.
- Interaction: five accessible steps, back/forward, local autosave, editable output, print/PDF, email, save, review CTA.
- AI: not required for the deterministic first version; future wording assistance is labeled AI-assisted.
- Lead capture: only at review/cross-device sharing.
- Limitation: output structures user input; it is not a recommendation.
- Phase: 1.

### Market Entry Compass / Pazara Giriş Pusulası — implemented prototype

- Need: compare opportunity and readiness without pretending that attractiveness alone determines entry.
- Input: two markets in prototype; editable weights and user-entered scores.
- Output: relative scorecard, accessible table, evidence gap, working-session CTA.
- Deterministic logic: weighted average; execution risk is inverted. AI cannot alter it.
- Full version adds 2–5 markets and twelve specified dimensions.
- Data classes are visibly separated: user assumption, approved Ganrich research, licensed external data, AI synthesis.
- Lead capture: after on-screen result.
- Limitation: no country data or default methodology claims.
- Phase: 1 prototype; full tool Phase 2.

### Scenario Studio / Senaryo Stüdyosu

- Need: test strategy against more than one plausible future.
- Input: decision, horizon, 2–4 uncertainties/ranges, drivers, constraints, alternatives, indicators.
- Output: up to four scenarios, implications, risks, opportunities, capabilities, warnings, now/conditional actions.
- Interaction: accessible quadrant and comparison table.
- Lead role: export or foresight workshop.
- AI: scenario naming/narrative only from entered and approved evidence; not a forecast.
- Privacy: no confidential data; explicit retention for saved work.
- Phase: 2.

### AI Value Lens / Yapay Zekâ Değer Merceği

- Need: identify AI opportunities through value, readiness, governance, and risk.
- Input: candidate use cases and ten assessment dimensions.
- Output: Act now / Validate / Build foundations / Monitor / Do not prioritize; dependencies, measures, governance, pilot questions.
- Scoring: deterministic and editable; methodology versioned.
- AI: assists synthesis, never technical certainty.
- Lead role: AI value workshop.
- Privacy: warning against confidential business data.
- Phase: 2.

### Transformation Pulse / Dönüşüm Nabzı

- Need: distinguish activity from value realization.
- Input: fourteen transformation-health dimensions.
- Output: internal diagnostic result, strengths, fragile areas, contradictions, bottlenecks, 30/60/90-day questions/actions.
- Scoring: no false benchmark; text labels accompany status.
- AI: synthesis of contradictions, with human review route.
- Lead role: transformation review.
- Phase: 2.

### Operating Model Lens / İşletim Modeli Merceği

- Need: identify operating-model friction without pretending to design a complete organization.
- Input: decisions, rights, structure, governance, processes, capability, accountabilities, data, measures, incentives, collaboration, geography, services, partners.
- Output: profile, friction, bottlenecks, duplication, gaps, principles, redesign questions, transition priorities.
- AI: preliminary synthesis only.
- Lead role: operating-model working session.
- Phase: 2.

### Portfolio Prioritizer / Portföy Önceliklendirme Aracı

- Need: decide what will not proceed.
- Input: initiatives and twelve dimensions.
- Output: value-feasibility, strategic contribution, resource demand, dependency view, sequencing, stop/start/continue, assumptions.
- Scoring: transparent deterministic weights.
- Lead role: prioritization workshop.
- Privacy: local guest mode; named portfolio may be sensitive.
- Phase: 2.

### Board Brief Builder / Yönetim Kurulu Brifingi Oluşturucu

- Need: prepare a decision-centered board discussion.
- Input: topic, objective, time, region, selected insights, depth, uncertainties, required decision.
- Output: issue, why now, evidence, alternatives, trade-offs, risks, board questions, decision, follow-up, sources in five formats.
- AI: visible labels for Ganrich publication, user input, and AI-assisted synthesis.
- Prohibitions: no fabricated finance or legal advice.
- Lead role: board briefing.
- Phase: 2.

### Insight Synthesis / İçgörü Sentezi

- Need: work across up to five selected Ganrich publications.
- Input: explicit selected source set and action.
- Output: recurring themes, contradictions, changes, region differences, executive synthesis, board questions, evidence.
- AI: may cite only selected sources; every claim retains a source link.
- Interaction: Brief me, Compare, Challenge, Regional implications, Board questions, Evidence, Save.
- Lead role: source-linked private briefing.
- Phase: 2.

## 19. Responsible AI architecture

1. Accept validated language-aware input.
2. Enforce input length, abuse controls, rate limits, and confidential-data warnings.
3. Retrieve only explicitly approved, published Ganrich content.
4. Preserve source ID, anchor, original language, publication/review dates, and eligibility.
5. Separate retrieved evidence, Ganrich interpretation, user input, deterministic score, and AI synthesis in the response schema.
6. Refuse when sources do not support an answer.
7. Log the exact source set for audit.
8. Do not train on visitor content without explicit consent.
9. Default to no conversation retention; disclose any enabled retention.
10. Offer human review.

Suggested response contract:

`{ supportStatus, confidence, synthesis, implications[], counterarguments[], sources[], selectedSourceIds[], userInputRefs[], deterministicResultRef, limitations[], humanReviewUrl }`

## 20. Privacy and security

- Guest personalization and tray storage remain device-local.
- Cross-device save and private sharing require identification and explicit retention disclosure.
- No confidential uploads in public tools.
- Secure server endpoints for forms; validation and anti-abuse controls.
- CRM receives only necessary context plus consent state.
- No optional marketing consent is preselected.
- Phase 3 requires authenticated owner/workspace access, noindex, session security, audit events, document malware scanning, storage lifecycle, and access revocation.
- Claims about certifications or controls require verification.

## 25. Final quality-control assessment

1. Every launched feature helps frame or understand a decision.
2. Provisional content and unavailable integrations are visibly labeled.
3. Useful results appear before identification.
4. Interfaces remain credible without animation.
5. English and Turkish are equally complete.
6. Evidence, interpretation, user input, deterministic output, and AI synthesis are separated.
7. No fabricated clients, outcomes, benchmarks, country data, finance, or legal advice.
8. AI has a defined source and audit boundary.
9. The experience is not reproducible from a generic consulting template.
10. Visual design remains calm, editorial, and boutique.
11. Interactive paths use native accessible controls.
12. No heavy charting or 3D libraries.
13. Tools recompose for mobile.
14. Every path can lead naturally to a senior conversation.
15. Later phases are not exposed as fake live capabilities.
16. Content governance makes maintenance ownership explicit.
